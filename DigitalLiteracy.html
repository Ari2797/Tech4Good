What is Digital Literacy?

Understanding the foundation of digital skills in the modern world

Beyond Basic Computer Skills

Digital literacy goes beyond knowing how to use a computer or smartphone. It encompasses the ability to find, evaluate, utilize, share, and create content using information technologies and the Internet.

A digitally literate person can perform tasks effectively in a digital environment, with the skills to navigate, communicate, and problem-solve using technology.

This includes understanding digital safety, recognizing credible information online, and using digital tools for learning, working, and socializing.

Key Components of Digital Literacy

The essential skills that form a comprehensive digital literacy foundation

Core Digital Competencies

Information Literacy: The ability to find, evaluate, and use digital information effectively and ethically.

Communication & Collaboration: Using digital tools to communicate, share, and work with others.

Digital Creation: Producing digital content, from documents and presentations to videos and websites.

Privacy & Safety: Understanding how to protect personal information and navigate online spaces securely.

Problem-Solving: Using digital tools to identify and solve problems in various contexts.
