What is Cybersecurity Awareness?

Understanding the importance of protecting your digital presence

More Than Just Strong Passwords

Cybersecurity awareness is the understanding of potential cyber threats and the knowledge of how to protect yourself and your organization from these threats. It involves recognizing risks and taking appropriate actions to prevent security breaches.

In today's digital age, cybersecurity is everyone's responsibility. From individuals to large organizations, understanding basic security principles is essential for protecting sensitive information.

Cyber threats continue to evolve, making continuous education and awareness crucial for maintaining digital safety.

Common Cyber Threats

Understanding the risks is the first step toward protection

Recognizing Digital Dangers

Phishing Attacks

Fraudulent attempts to obtain sensitive information by disguising as a trustworthy entity in electronic communication.

Ransomware

Malicious software that blocks access to a computer system until a sum of money is paid.

Identity Theft

The fraudulent acquisition and use of a person's private identifying information, usually for financial gain.
